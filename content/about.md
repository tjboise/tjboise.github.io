I'm Tianjie Zhang, based at Rutgers University.

I started taking photos to remember things, and kept going because I liked paying attention. I mostly shoot whatever is in front of me: streets, trips, and quiet everyday scenes. I'm not after the perfect shot, just a moment that feels real.

This site is where I keep my favorites, grouped by year. The layout is adapted from [leovan.me](https://github.com/leovan/leovan.me).
